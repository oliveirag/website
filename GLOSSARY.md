# Website

Guilherme Oliveira's personal site at notgui.com: who he is, what he builds, what he writes and films.

## Language

### Scenes

**Intro**:
The opening screen where the name grows flowers on an empty sky. It ends when the visitor clicks, taps or presses a key.
_Avoid_: Splash, loader, landing

**Field**:
The grass scene pinned to the bottom of the screen, under the Sky. It is always visible and nothing touches it.
_Avoid_: Hero, landing page, background

**Sky**:
The area above the Field where page content sits and scrolls, fading out before it reaches the grass.
_Avoid_: Content area, page body

**Night**:
The dark theme, shown as the Field after dark. It is the default.
_Avoid_: Dark mode

**Day**:
The light theme, shown as the Field in daylight.
_Avoid_: Light mode

### Pages

**Home**:
The page that opens with the Intro and then shows About and Projects in the Sky.
_Avoid_: Landing page, index

**About**:
The block on Home with the bio, contact links, tech stack, photo, resume download and Now Playing.

**Projects**:
The section on Home listing featured work, followed by Commits.

**Commits**:
The yearly GitHub commit count and per-repository ranking shown under Projects.
_Avoid_: Contributions, git stats

**Now Playing**:
The line above the photo in About showing the track playing on Spotify right now. It disappears when nothing is playing.
_Avoid_: Spotify widget

**Chatbot**:
GuiBot, the chat assistant tuned to answer as Guilherme.

**Top 5**:
The ranked lists of favourite movies, anime and video games.
_Avoid_: Bookshelf, other

**Thoughts**:
Dated posts written in the moment, short or long, listed newest first.
_Avoid_: Blog, articles, writing

**Post menu**:
The list a "..." button beside a Thoughts post's title opens, for sharing the post or reading it as plain text: copying its link, copying or viewing it as Markdown, asking ChatGPT or Claude about it, and sharing a selected quote.
_Avoid_: Share menu, kebab menu, more menu

**Videos**:
The page that lists Guilherme's YouTube videos and vlogs.
_Avoid_: Vlogs

**Classic site**:
The site as it was before the revamp, kept reachable from About. The link reads "click here for old site".
_Avoid_: Legacy, v1

### Sound

**Ambience**:
The calm looping nature sound that plays while the site is open and unmuted.
_Avoid_: Background music, soundtrack

**Cue**:
A short sound tied to one action: following a link, switching theme, or sending a Chatbot message.
_Avoid_: Sound effect, SFX
